## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- Bundle identifier is now `dev.gwennha.Cadastre` (was `local.cadastre`).
  macOS treats it as a new app: Full Disk Access and the folder permissions
  have to be granted again, and the recent folders start empty. Markers are
  unaffected — they live in `~/Library/Application Support/Cadastre`.
- The app icon is generated by `outils/icone.swift` (charter family "tools")
  and shipped as `Resources/AppIcon.icns`, instead of being drawn by the
  binary at packaging time.
- Build through the charter's `Makefile`: `make build`, `make test`,
  `make package` (→ `build/Cadastre.app`). `Scripts/bundle.sh` became
  `outils/package.sh`; `Scripts/verify.sh` became `outils/verify.sh`;
  `Scripts/release.sh` is replaced by `make sign` and `make dmg`.

## [0.2.0] — 2026-09-19

Never published; recorded for history.

### Added
- Whole-disk mode (`⌘⇧O`, `--disk`): the Data volume, framed by the
  container's other volumes.
- "What changed" panel (`⌘D`, `--open changes`), follow changes (`⌘⇧L`),
  Quick Look on the selection, undo for moves to the Trash (`⌘Z`).
- VoiceOver: the map's first two levels are exposed as buttons.
- French and English throughout, units and dates included.
- Wider reclaim catalogue; nine unit tests.
